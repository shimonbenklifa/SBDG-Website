01 — The Firm

Founded in 2010, SB Development Group is a vertically integrated developer and general contractor — pairing institutional discipline with the hands-on craft of a builder.

Led by founders Joseph Stern and Roni Benjamini, whose backgrounds span engineering, construction, development, acquisitions, and finance, SBD controls every stage of the lifecycle. Through its affiliate, 1 Oak Construction, the firm builds what it imagines — eliminating the gap between vision and execution.

From luxury condominiums and high-rise towers to mixed-use and residential communities, SBD has delivered more than two dozen ground-up projects across New York and Florida — and is now shaping the next generation of the South Florida skyline.
