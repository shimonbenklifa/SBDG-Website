01 / The Firm

Founded in 2010, SB Development Group is a vertically integrated developer and general contractor. We pair institutional discipline with the craft of a builder.

Led by founders Joseph Stern and Roni Benjamini, whose backgrounds span engineering, construction, development, acquisitions, and finance, the firm controls every stage of a project. We build what we develop, closing the gap between vision and execution.

From luxury condominiums and high rise towers to mixed use and residential communities, SB Development has delivered more than two dozen ground up projects across New York and Florida, with a growing presence in South Florida.
